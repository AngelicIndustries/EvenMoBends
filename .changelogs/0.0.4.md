* Skin layers, flat or 3D, are now drawn through Salamander's skin layer API; needs Salamander 0.0.2. Wawel Auth stays optional, for skins from players' own providers
* The Aether's accessories follow the bones: gloves, pendant, shield, cape, wings, halo, and agility boots with their wings
* Other mods' armor models follow the bones, including Avaritia's infinity armor with all its effects
* Adventure Backpack's backpacks, copter pack and coal jetpack ride the body, and flying on the copter pack or jetpack uses the flight pose
* Armor stays cut at elbows and knees when a shader draws it, such as Avaritia's cosmic overlay, and skin-tight overlays such as its full-set shimmer fit slim arms
* SimpleSkinBackport no longer switches players to its default skin's arm shape
* Wall Grab and Tuck & Roll poses on the bones
* Swimming with the Mo' Bends stroke under Aqua Acrobatics
* Holding a block leaves the legs alone
* Sword combos and other timed animations play right after a respawn or dimension change
* Players with Ears keep animating
* Clients without Even Mo' Bends can join servers with it
* Et Futurum Requiem's husks animate as zombies, with their own texture and size
* Special Mobs' zombies, zombie pigmen, spiders and cave spiders animate as the vanilla ones, with their own textures, eyes and sizes
* Settings moved to a Forge config screen from the mods list, also from the main menu: General and Animations, which opens the mob menu; the G key and the menu's Settings page are gone
* The mob menu was redone: it fits small windows, a player spins on its first page playing a new animation every 10 seconds, and the Blockbench command is shown as a command you can click to copy
* The menu's Player page lists the player's animations: click one to preview it, and its switch to turn it on or off at once
* Menu previews animate smoothly, also while the game is paused, and no longer show the backs of heads and bodies over their fronts when opened from the main menu
* Face-only player packs such as Just Expressions no longer replace Mo' Bends: Mo' Bends animates the body and the pack's face blinks and looks around on Mo' Bends' head (needs Salamander 0.0.3). Packs that change the body, such as Fresh Moves, still replace the player; the General setting "Combine face-only player packs" turns this off
* The mob menu says when something else draws a mob in Mo' Bends' place: a resource pack's model, naming the pack, another mod's renderer, Smart Moving, or the mob's Animate switch being off
* Players no longer take the fighting stance for 3 seconds after joining a world, nor in other mods' previews such as Wawel Auth's account manager
* The expression editor moved to Salamander, which draws the eyes and brows it prepares; it opens from Salamander's settings
